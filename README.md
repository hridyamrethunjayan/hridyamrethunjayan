## `03 / THE WAY OF BUILDING`

```text
OBSERVE
   |
   v
QUESTION WHAT EVERYONE ASSUMES
   |
   v
BUILD SOMETHING THAT TESTS THE IDEA
   |
   v
FIND WHAT DOESN'T WORK
   |
   v
UNDERSTAND WHY
   |
   v
BUILD AGAIN
   |
   +--------> REPEAT
```

I value fundamentals over shortcuts, experimentation over assumptions, and understanding over appearances.

The objective isn't simply to make something work.

It's to understand why it works, when it fails, and what could make it better.
